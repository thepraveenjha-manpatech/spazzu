Questions

Before you ask

How do I actually use it?

Shake the can. Spray an even layer of foam over the dirty areas. Work it in with a soft brush, then wipe the foam off with a dry microfibre cloth and let the shoes air dry.

What can I use it on?

The pack says it is safe for all sneaker materials. As with any cleaner, try a small hidden patch first on anything delicate.

Why not just put them in the washing machine?

A machine soaks the whole shoe, which is hard on glue and on the shape, and then you lose a day to drying. A foam stays on the surface you are cleaning, so you wipe it off and wear them.

Do I need water?

No bucket and no soaking. Spray, brush, then wipe off with a dry cloth.
